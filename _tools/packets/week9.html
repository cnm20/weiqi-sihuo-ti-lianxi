        • Dieter says:
          October 28, 2011 at 8:40 pm

          An analysis tree splits into branches all of which have a certain number of moves in total. The total number of branches is my “width” and the maximum number of moves for any branch is my “depth”.

          See http://senseis.xmp.net/?ReadingDepth and http://senseis.xmp.net/?ReadingWidth

          Since we’ve been discussing the actual difficulty of the problems, I thought I’d provide these metrics instead of a gut feeling, but of course these metrics highly depend on one’s strength. E.g. for a beginner the depth of reading out a bulky five can be as much as 13, while for us it’s 1.

          Still, it may be interesting for a problem composer to know how many variations I as a 1-2d bother to verify and how deep I’ll go before cutting off the analysis, so I keep publishing my answers this way.
